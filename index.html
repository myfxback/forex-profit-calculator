How to Calculate Forex Profit

Working out your forex profit comes down to one simple relationship: how many pips the price moved, multiplied by what each pip is worth, multiplied by your position size. This forex profit calculator handles that math instantly, but it helps to understand what's happening behind the numbers.

Profit = Pips × Pip Value × Lot Size

On a Buy (Long) trade, you profit when price rises — pips are measured as Exit minus Entry. On a Sell (Short) trade, you profit when price falls — pips are measured as Entry minus Exit. Getting the direction right matters, since it flips whether your result shows a profit or a loss.

For most USD-quoted major pairs like EUR/USD or GBP/USD, one pip on a standard lot (100,000 units) is worth approximately $10. That value scales down proportionally on mini lots (0.10) and micro lots (0.01), which is why position size is just as important as the price move itself when estimating your forex profit and loss.

Keep in mind this forex profit loss calculator shows your gross result based on price movement alone — spread, commission, and overnight swap fees still apply on top and will affect your actual net return.
